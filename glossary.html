Shared glossary

Vocabulary for the D(S₃) preparation lessons. Definitions follow Lo et al.'s pedagogical notes and the preparation theory.

D(S₃) ground state:
A state satisfying all ground-state vertex and plaquette projectors of the S₃ quantum double model.
Avoid: A unique ground state when no logical sector is specified.

Charge conjugation:
The order-two symmetry reversing the three-valued qutrit label, so that zero stays fixed and one exchanges with two.

Gauging:
Promotion of a global symmetry to a local gauge symmetry by introducing gauge degrees of freedom and imposing local constraints.

Feed-forward:
A classical choice of subsequent quantum operations based on earlier measurement outcomes.
Avoid: Postselection when the measured branch is corrected and retained.

Logical sector:
A choice of global logical properties among states satisfying the same local ground-state constraints.

Heralding:
Selection of experimental samples using additional measurement outcomes that signal whether a desired condition holds.
Avoid: Feed-forward when a sample is accepted or rejected rather than corrected.
